Flip Clock Focus – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Flip Clock Focus (“the app”, package com.hourflip.app) is a flip clock with an hourly activity tracker, a pomodoro focus lock, to-do lists and a screen-time view. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Summary

The app has no account, no ads and no analytics. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Hourly track entries (time, your text, status), to-do items, pomodoro sessions and app settings are stored in a local database on your phone.
  • This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. You can export it yourself (CSV / JSON) using the share sheet; where it goes is your choice.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen notifications: to ring the hourly chime at the times you choose and show the “What did you do?” popup, also on the lock screen.
  • Usage access: during a focus session the app checks which app is in front, to bring you back to the timer; it is also used to show you your own screen time. This information is processed on the device only and never stored outside it or sent anywhere.
  • Display over other apps, foreground service: to keep the focus lock running and return to the timer.
  • Phone state: to detect an active call so the focus lock never interrupts it.
  • Microphone (optional): only while you tap the mic to speak your answer; speech is converted to text by your device’s speech service. The app does not record or keep audio.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
